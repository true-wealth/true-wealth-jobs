# Product Owner (80–100%)

**Position code:** VAC-05
**Contract:** Permanent
**Workload:** 80–100%
**Location:** Zürich Binz (two S-Bahn stops from Zürich HB); hybrid, with the
majority of the week together in the office

**True Wealth** is the Swiss pioneer in online wealth management and pillar 3a
investing, experiencing rapid growth. With over 50,000 clients entrusting us with
their savings and investments, we are committed to making investing simpler, more
transparent, and more accessible. We pass on the advantages of digitalization to
our clients, enabling them to invest and provide for their retirement as
efficiently as possible.

To meet the rising popularity of our investment solution, ensure the high quality
of our product, and continue innovating the way wealth management is done, you
will take ownership in this important Product Owner role. You will translate our
product strategy — owned by the CEO and the product leadership team — into
well-defined, shipped product initiatives, closely interacting with your Product
Owner peer, developers, UX/UI designers, marketing and management, as well as
external stakeholders. The role reports to the CEO.

---

## Your Responsibilities

- Take ownership of product initiatives and drive them from initiation through
  definition, implementation, and release, collaborating effectively with the
  other PO, designers, engineers, compliance, and marketing colleagues.
- Identify and understand customer needs and translate them into scalable and
  delightful product features; strive to continuously improve our offering.
- Analyse, define, and document business requirements — including regulatory
  requirements — and push for solutions that provide an excellent user experience.
- Guide sprint execution together with the product leadership team; maintain a
  high-quality product backlog together with the other PO and the engineers.
- Interact with institutional clients and partners to manage dependencies,
  change requests, and bug fixes.

---

## Your Profile

**Required**
- Work experience as a Product Owner or in a similar function in the digital space.
- Strong business analysis and conceptual skills with attention to the necessary
  details; a data-driven approach to decision making.
- Ability to communicate ideas and plans effectively and create shared
  understanding in the team.
- Ability to manage projects, keep timelines, and communicate with stakeholders.
- High-agency spirit; hands-on, curious, and flexible mindset.
- Intrinsically motivated to get things right — from the initiative brief down to
  the fine details of a shipped feature.
- Fluent in both English and German.

**Nice to have**
- Prior experience in the fintech space.
- General knowledge of asset management, the regulatory environment of wealth
  management, or occupational pensions (3a, FZG, 1e).
