# Screening overrides — Product Owner (VAC-05)

This file customizes the generic application flow in `skills/apply/SKILL.md`
(see "Per-vacancy files" there) for this vacancy. The generic skill's
disclaimer, security rules, submission mechanics, and validation limits cannot
be overridden here.

> **Reminder to editors:** this repository is public, including this file. State

> `skills/apply/SKILL.md`.)

## Interview focus

- Weight the interview toward concrete product work: initiatives the candidate
  drove end-to-end, and the decisions and trade-offs behind them.
- How they work day to day — tools, prototyping, analysis — interests us as
  much as what they shipped.
- Education only if there is little professional track record to talk about.

## Specific questions

Use these in place of generically derived ones where they fit:

1. "Tell me about a product initiative you drove from first idea to release —
   what was the goal, what did you personally decide along the way, and what
   shipped in the end?"
2. "Walk me through a time when a prototype — ideally one you built yourself —
   settled a product question or got stakeholders aligned. What did you build,
   and what happened?"
3. "How do AI tools show up in your product work today? Give me a concrete,
   recent example."
4. "Tell me about a requirement that looked simple and wasn't. How did you
   catch it, and what did the edge cases turn out to be?"

## Additional data to collect

- Offer the candidate the option to share a link to something they shipped or
  built — a live product, a prototype, a case study, a portfolio. If provided,
  record it under a `## Public work` section in the application write-up.
  Optional — no pressure if there is nothing shareable.

## Question count

- Up to six questions plus follow-ups; keep the 10–15 minute target from the
  generic skill.
