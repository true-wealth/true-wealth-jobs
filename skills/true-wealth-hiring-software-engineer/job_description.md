# Software Engineer / Senior Software Engineer

**Position code:** VAC-06
**Contract:** Permanent
**Workload:** 80–100%
**Location:** Zürich Binz (two S-Bahn stops from Zürich HB); flexible hybrid work
policy

**True Wealth** is the Swiss pioneer in online wealth management and pillar 3a
investing, experiencing rapid growth. With 50,000 clients entrusting us with their
savings and investments, we are committed to making investing simpler, more
transparent, and more accessible. Our mission is to deliver exceptional value by
combining digitization with cost-efficiency, empowering our clients to take
control of their financial future.

We're looking for an excellent Software Engineer to join our engineering team. We
build the critical systems that run the business, from the trading and portfolio
engine, to the CRM and compliance tools, to the marketing machinery. You will work
full stack, on critical backend services and delightful user interfaces. Together
with fellow engineers, product owners, designers, and other business roles, you
will improve our service and innovate on the product.

---

## Your Responsibilities

- Own technical design, implementation, test automation, and release of small to
  large product features.
- Improve the product infrastructure and engineering systems.
- Respond to livesite alerts and client support inquiries to ensure high quality
  of service.
- Take ownership of components and business domains over time.
- Contribute to a productive and healthy team culture.

---

## Your Profile

This role is open to strong engineers at any stage — from a few years in to
seasoned seniors. What matters most is judgment and craft, not tenure.

**Required**
- Strong engineering fundamentals: an engineering/CS degree or clearly
  demonstrated equivalent skill.
- A track record of shipping something real, through professional experience,
  internships, open-source work, or serious side projects.
- Solid grounding in a general-purpose programming language, such as Java.
- Sharp technical judgment: you can read code you didn't write and spot bugs,
  wrong assumptions, or questionable design choices.
- Adaptable and pragmatic: you are not attached to a particular toolchain and can
  balance the desire for perfect code with the realities of shipping product.
- Team player, reliable and accountable.
- Fluency in English.

**Nice to have**
- Business domain knowledge from finance, trading, portfolio management, or
  accounting.
- German. Based in or around Zürich.
