## Interview language

- Offer to run the interview in German or English, whichever the candidate
  prefers. Record the language used in *Process notes*.

## Interview focus

- Weight the interview toward concrete expert work: hard tax or pension cases
  the candidate resolved, how the question reached them, and what they decided.
- Cover both lines of the role — tax, and financial/pension planning — rather
  than only one.
- Ask how the candidate documents or hands over expert knowledge so that others
  can apply it: written rules, checklists, worked cases, training material.

## Specific questions

Use these in place of generically derived ones where they fit:

1. "Tell me about a hard tax or pension case that was escalated to you — what
   made it hard, what you decided, and how you explained the outcome to the
   client or the adviser."
2. "Walk me through a situation where you had to turn your own judgement into a
   written rule or procedure that others then applied. What did you write, and
   what happened when a case did not fit it?"
3. "How do you keep your knowledge of cantonal and communal tax rules current
   across cantons? Give a recent example of a change you had to work through."
4. "How do modern tools — including AI assistants — show up in your professional
   work today, if at all? Give a recent concrete example, or tell us why not."

## Additional data to collect

Record these under a `## Qualifications and engagement` section in the
application write-up (they are facts the job description asks for, not new
payload fields):

- Which of the two professional qualifications the candidate holds, with the
  exact title of each and the year obtained — tax (*eidg. dipl. Steuerexperte/-expertin*
  or *Treuhänder/-in mit eidg. Fachausweis*) and financial & pension planning
  (*Experte/Expertin in Finanz- und Vorsorgeplanung mit eidg. Diplom* or
  *Finanzplaner/-in mit eidg. Fachausweis*). If a qualification is in progress,
  record the expected date.
- The engagement the candidate has in mind: full-time, or fractional / advisory
  — and, if fractional, the capacity they could commit (days per week) and any
  current role or mandate they would keep alongside.
- Languages, including whether the candidate speaks or understands Swiss German
  (record under the usual `## Languages` section).
- Optional: the cantons the candidate has worked with most.

## Question count

- Up to six questions plus follow-ups is fine for this role; keep the 10–15
  minute target from the generic skill.
