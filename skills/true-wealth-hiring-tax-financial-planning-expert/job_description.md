# Tax & Financial-Planning Expert (100% or fractional)

**Position code:** VAC-09
**Contract:** Permanent, or a fractional / advisory arrangement (see "Engagement model")
**Workload:** 100%, or fractional
**Location:** Zürich Binz (two S-Bahn stops from Zürich HB); hybrid, with about

**Reports to:** CEO

---

## About True Wealth

True Wealth is one of Switzerland's leading digital wealth managers — CHF 3.4bn
under management, 50,000 clients, with a FINMA licence as an asset manager
including assets of occupational pension schemes. We are building a digital
financial- and retirement-planning service inside the True Wealth app, and we
are looking for the expert who will own the quality of the advice it gives.

---

## The role

You are the authority on whether a financial plan is *right*. You define what
sound advice looks like, validate it, and stand behind it. Your expertise is
what lets us deliver digital financial plans that our clients — and we — can
rely on. This is a substantive, senior role at the heart of a new product, not
a back-office review function.

---

## What you will own

- **Define correct vs. incorrect advice.** Author and maintain the worked,
  validated reference cases that set our standard for sound planning.
- **Curate the planning knowledge base.** Own the tax and pension knowledge the
  service is built on, and its annual update across federal, cantonal, and
  communal rules — all 26 cantons.
- **Quality-assure the output.** Review the plans the service produces, diagnose
  where they fall short, and raise the standard.
- **Own the rationale.** Ensure every recommendation can be explained to a
  client in plain language — why *this* course, for *this* person.
- **Validate the tax logic.** Check the cantonal tax computations against each
  canton's official calculation, and specify the logic precisely enough to be
  implemented and tested.
- **Meet the bar.** Ensure our advice meets the relevant regulatory and
  professional standards.

Depending on your profile and ambition, you may also own procurement of the tax
and financial data the service relies on.

---

## Your profile

You combine two distinct lines of Swiss professional authority.

**Required**
- **Tax:** *eidg. dipl. Steuerexperte/-expertin* — or, at minimum, *Treuhänder/-in
  mit eidg. Fachausweis*.
- **Financial & pension planning:** *Experte/Expertin in Finanz- und
  Vorsorgeplanung mit eidg. Diplom* (höhere Fachprüfung) — or, at minimum,
  *Finanzplaner/-in mit eidg. Fachausweis*.
- Experience as **the tax reference — second- or third-level support — inside a
  financial-planning or private-banking function**: the person colleagues
  escalate the hard cases to.
- **Rigour and precision.** You can turn judgement into explicit, testable rules
  rather than intuition that lives only in your head.
- **Fluent German and English.**

**We also value**
- A **legal background** or strong legal fluency in Swiss tax and pension law.
- **Quantitative and structural acumen** — comfort with the mathematical and
  structural reality of what financial planning boils down to: models,
  parameters, and edge cases, and the discipline to specify them unambiguously.
- **Comfort working alongside modern tooling and engineers.** You are curious
  about how our systems reason, and you want to shape the product — not
  supervise it grudgingly.
- **Swiss German** — a plus, not a requirement.
- Familiarity with US withholding-tax and QI/FATCA documentation requirements.

You do *not* need to have launched a digital product, and you do not own the
investment models or portfolio approach (those sit elsewhere in the firm). You
need deep, defensible expertise and the will to make it usable at scale.
