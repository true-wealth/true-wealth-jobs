# Senior Front-end Engineer (80–100%)

**Position code:** VAC-07
**Contract:** Permanent
**Workload:** 80–100%
**Location:** Zürich Binz (two S-Bahn stops from Zürich HB); hybrid, with about
half of the week together in the office

**True Wealth** is the Swiss pioneer in online wealth management and pillar 3a
investing, experiencing rapid growth. With over 50,000 clients entrusting us with
their savings and investments, we are committed to making investing simpler and
more transparent — without bloated management fees. Our mission is to deliver
exceptional value by combining digitization with cost-efficiency, empowering our
clients to take control of their financial future.

We're looking for an excellent **Senior Front-end Engineer** to join our
engineering team. We build the critical systems that run the business, from the
customer dashboard, to the onboarding flows and trading tools, to the marketing
machinery. You will work on our end-user application and the marketing website.
Together with fellow engineers, product owners, designers, and marketing
colleagues, you will improve our service and innovate on the product.

---

## Your Responsibilities

- Own technical design, implementation, test automation, and release of small to
  large product features and marketing website updates.
- Improve the front-end architecture and the marketing website tool stack.
- Own the marketing website's Core Web Vitals and SEO.
- Work with designers and product owners to turn concepts into interfaces that
  feel obvious to use.
- Contribute to a productive and healthy team culture.

---

## Your Profile

We're hiring for judgment and craft rather than tenure, but this role expects
someone who works fully autonomously.

**Required**
- Strong engineering fundamentals: an engineering/CS degree or clearly
  demonstrated equivalent.
- Proven experience building, shipping, and maintaining production web
  applications at scale.
- An eye for detail that extends past the happy path: layout responsiveness,
  interaction bugs, loading/error states, and the awkward edge cases real users
  hit.
- Sharp technical judgment: you can read code you didn't write and spot bugs,
  wrong assumptions, or questionable design choices.
- Adaptable and pragmatic: you are not attached to a particular toolchain and can
  balance the desire for perfect code with the realities of shipping product.
- Team player, reliable and accountable.
- Fluency in English.

**Nice to have**
- Business domain knowledge from finance, trading, portfolio management, or
  accounting.
- German.
- Based in or around Zürich.
